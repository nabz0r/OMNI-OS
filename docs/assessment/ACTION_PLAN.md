# OMNI success action plan

Baseline: 24 September 2026. These are proposed actions with acceptance criteria, not completed work or guaranteed outcomes. Owners are roles to assign to real people. The audit and documents are complete; company-building and customer-validation actions remain open.

Priority definitions: **P0** removes a threat to the first credible application or pilot. **P1** makes the pilot repeatable and supportable. **P2** is justified only after early evidence. Effort estimates are planning ranges for a competent, available team, not quotes, promises or additive calendar durations.

## P0: decide, substantiate and make the first deployment trustworthy

| ID | Action and accountable role | Suggested timing / effort | Dependency | Evidence required to close |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A01 | Establish real founder, team, company, ownership, IP and cash facts. Founder with finance/legal support | Days 1-14; 2-5 days plus external turnaround | Access to actual records | Private fact register reconciled to supporting documents; no guessed application fields |
| A02 | Choose one buyer and one recurring documentation job. Founder | Days 1-7; 1-2 days | Initial target criteria | One-page scope naming buyer, user, approver, existing alternative, supported devices and excluded data |
| A03 | Run 20 problem interviews and qualify a first 50-account list. Founder | Days 1-45; 8-12 working days | A02; legitimate outreach authority | Dated notes, refusals, buying process and at least five independent accounts with the same recurring job, or a documented pivot |
| A04 | Define personal versus work authority. Product owner and security lead | Days 1-21; 3-5 days | A02 and initial buyer feedback | Matrix of who owns, views, grants, administers, exports, retains and deletes each data class; pilot forbids mixed personal content |
| A05 | Design and implement supported encrypted recovery. Product engineer | Design in first 30 days; implementation estimate 2-6 weeks after design | A04, platform scope and assigned engineer | Restore on declared paths; lost/invalid key and interrupted restore fail safely; no plaintext fallback; documented recovery limits |
| A06 | Establish trusted release and update identity. Release owner | Before ordinary paid deployment; 1-3 weeks plus vendor lead time | Platform credentials, device access and budget | Signed/notarized delivery where applicable; clean-device install and upgrade preserve data; rollback and support lifetime documented |
| A07 | Replace illustrative cash and costs with actuals and quotes. Founder/finance | Days 1-21; 2-4 days | A01; supplier scopes | Reconciled cash/liabilities, compensation basis, review quote, monthly cash plan and explicit unfunded fallback |
| A08 | Finalise truthful programme applications. Founder | YC internal review 26 Oct; Nexus 9 Nov | A01, genuine video and current live fields | Consistent answers, team slide and terms review; submission only from the authorised account with receipt |

Do not treat A05 as complete because a database file can be copied. Do not treat A06 as complete because a checksum matches. The first concerns recoverable key custody; the second concerns identity, distribution and safe lifecycle operations.

## Decision calendar

- **By 8 October 2026:** scope, actual founder/corporate fact status, five interviews, work-authority draft and cost/recovery owners identified.
- **By 24 October:** fifteen interviews targeted, four demonstrations, recovery design, signing/review quotes and a documented pilot offer. YC final review target remains 26 October.
- **By 23 November:** twenty interviews and eight demonstrations targeted; applications checked against actual deadlines; relevant implementation work proceeds only within available resources.
- **By 23 December:** three pilot commitments targeted, with a clear distinction between interest, signed contract and money collected. Make a continue/narrow/pivot decision based on evidence.

These are proposed internal dates, not scheduled reminders or programme rules. Official YC and Nexus cutoffs are recorded in the [programme register](../funding/PROGRAMMES.md).

## Operating dashboard

Use a weekly sheet or CRM with these definitions before collecting data. Do not make optional product telemetry a prerequisite for participation.

| Measure | Definition | Review decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified account | Recurring job, buyer, data approval, supported device and buying path established | Do not count an unqualified contact as pipeline |
| Activation | First approved useful task completed | Inspect setup failure before acquiring more users |
| Week-four useful use | Activated participants with at least one agreed useful task in week four / all activated participants | Target at least 70% in the pilot; report numerator and denominator |
| Net task time | Baseline time minus OMNI time, including setup allocation, review and correction | Do not equate context compression with productivity |
| Task success | Predefined quality rubric, same model/budget and blinded review where feasible | Quality loss can override time saved |
| Disclosure failures | Unexpected provider receipt on a denied or revoked path | Pause affected distribution immediately |
| Support cost | Direct hours at an explicit loaded rate, plus direct infrastructure/payment cost | Compare with the price before scaling |
| Paid continuation | Agreed recurring service with cash collected | Separate intent, invoice and payment |
| Cash runway | Forecast using reconciled cash, liabilities and committed receipts | Reforecast monthly; separate pending grants and investor discussions |

After two focused iterations without useful gains, stop expanding the segment and revisit the workflow. After three pilots without repeat willingness to pay, diagnose value, buyer, pricing and support before raising acquisition spend. These are management rules, not a guarantee of success.
