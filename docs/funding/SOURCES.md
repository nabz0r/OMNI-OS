# Research source register

All sources accessed on 24 September 2026. Organiser pages are primary sources. Product vendor descriptions are not independent comparative tests. The roadmap and financial plan contain explicitly labelled author assumptions, not external statistics.

## S01 Nexus 2027 awards

[Official source](https://nexusluxembourg.com/startups-awards-2027)

Open 10 July to 16 November 2026, noon CET. Selection 10 December. Event 9-10 March 2027. Selected-company package EUR 99 VAT included, non-refundable. Prize advertised as value over EUR 125,000; cash split unverified.

## S02 Nexus application portal

[Official source](https://nexuslux.co/startup)

Browser confirmed 2027 heading and 16-step application. First step requires SVG/AI/EPS logo, transparent PNG and PDF deck up to 20 MB. YouTube video link optional. Later steps not completed.

## S03 YC application

[Official source](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply)

Winter 2027, January-March in San Francisco. On-time deadline 2 November 2026 20:00 PT. Decision by 11 December for on-time applicants. Future-batch early decision offered.

## S04 YC deal

[Official source](https://www.ycombinator.com/deal)

USD 500,000: USD 125,000 for 7% and USD 375,000 uncapped MFN SAFE. US, Canadian, Cayman or Singapore parent required for investment. Luxembourg structure may need reorganisation.

## S05 YC founder video

[Official source](https://www.ycombinator.com/video)

One minute, founders speaking, all founders included. Use talking points rather than a recited script. Product demo separate.

## S06 Fit 4 Start current status

[Official source](https://luxinnovation.lu/news/fit-4-start-17-a-new-participation-record)

Edition 17 closed 10 July 2026. Selected teams start coaching January 2027. Announcement describes EUR 50,000 plus up to EUR 100,000 conditional aid.

## S07 Fit 4 Start eligibility

[Official source](https://luxinnovation.lu/assess-and-accelerate/fit4start/eligibility)

At least two people, one full-time and attending sessions while developing Luxembourg operations. No incorporation required to apply. Existing company at most five years old and small-enterprise criteria. Participants at least 18.

## S08 Fit 4 Start 18 pre-registration

[Official source](https://luxinnovation.lu/fit-4-start-pre-register-now)

Notification form available. No application deadline or cohort calendar shown.

## S09 Fit 4 Start public funding page

[Official source](https://guichet.public.lu/en/entreprises/financement-aides/regime-fit-4/fit-4-start.html)

General maximum EUR 150,000 in EUR 50,000/80,000/20,000 tranches. Aid filing via authenticated MyGuichet. Downloadable detailed documents on this page explicitly refer to edition 16.

## S10 Fit 4 Start edition 16 reference guidelines

[Official source](https://guichet.public.lu/dam-assets/catalogue-pdf/aides-meco/fit-4-start/programme-candidature/lignes-directices-programme-candidature-en.pdf)

Historical edition-specific capital, expenditure and reporting conditions. Calendar starts January 2026. Must not reuse its 2027 dates as edition 18 deadlines.

## S11 LHoFT incubation

[Official source](https://lhoft.com/services/)

Fintech incubation, workspace and soft landing. Connections to financial services ecosystem. No guaranteed cash award or dated admission deadline verified.

## S12 Technoport incubation

[Official source](https://technoport.lu/programs/incubation-program)

Technology incubation for validated concepts, with pre-incubation route for earlier projects. No guaranteed funding or dated intake verified.

## S13 Techstars terms

[Official source](https://www.techstars.com/investment-terms)

Standard USD 220,000: USD 20,000 CEA for 5% plus USD 200,000 uncapped MFN SAFE. Asia-Pacific exception USD 120,000 total. Programme and investment terms must be checked before acceptance.

## S14 Techstars programmes

[Official source](https://www.techstars.com/locations)

Amsterdam ABN AMRO Future of Finance and Anywhere listed. Specific 2027 cohort deadlines not verified.

## S15 EIC Accelerator

[Official source](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)

Targets high-risk market-creating innovations at TRL 6-8. Grant below EUR 2.5m and investment up to EUR 10m described for current framework. 2027 call terms not verified.

## S16 CSSF DORA guidance

[Official source](https://www.cssf.lu/en/ict-and-cyber-risk-for-dora-entities/)

DORA applies to covered financial entities since 17 January 2025; ICT risk, incident management, testing and third-party oversight are material procurement topics.

## S17 CSSF ICT service arrangements

[Official source](https://www.cssf.lu/en/2025/04/definition-of-ict-services-under-dora-new-forms-for-ict-third-party-arrangements-ict-outsourcing-arrangements/)

Service classification and criticality determine relevant ICT third-party and outsourcing processes. Do not claim a product certification from this framework.

## S18 European Commission AI Act overview

[Official source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)

Use-case and role assessment required. Timeline is evolving and must be checked against operative law at deployment; no blanket 2027 product compliance claim.

## S19 GDPR official text
