## Short description

Local AI memory with explicit sharing controls.

## Product description

OMNI helps people reuse context across supported AI tools while controlling what each provider receives. Users review and correct memories in an encrypted local vault, grant selected context to a destination, inspect disclosure receipts and revoke future access. Request policies check prompts, history and supported text attachments before provider egress. A macOS and Android evaluation MVP is available. We propose to test the product with small fintech engineering and operations teams, beginning with synthetic and approved non-sensitive workflows. Commercial demand, productivity gains and enterprise deployment requirements remain to be validated.

## Problem

Professionals working across AI tools repeatedly rebuild project context. Teams also need to understand which information leaves their environment. Manual context briefs are simple but require ongoing effort, while provider-specific memory ties continuity to one tool. OMNI tests whether a local, reviewable memory layer with explicit sharing permissions can reduce that effort without weakening user control.

## Innovation and distinction

OMNI combines an end-user memory workflow with deterministic checks before supported requests reach a model. The local vault stores provenance and corrections. Sharing grants specify a destination and scope, and disclosure receipts make the result inspectable. The open-source implementation allows technical review. The product does not yet provide fleet management, system-wide enforcement or zero-knowledge proof analytics. Its advantage must be demonstrated against a manual context brief and existing gateway or memory tools.

## Business model

The proposed first offer is a EUR 2,000 assisted four-week pilot for up to ten users, followed by testing a EUR 500 monthly organisation subscription. Customers supply and pay for their chosen model providers. Pricing and conversion remain unvalidated, and billing is not implemented. Revenue would come from maintained integrations, deployment and support rather than selling personal profiles.

## Why Nexus and what we seek

We want to meet technical and security leaders who can evaluate a narrowly scoped AI workflow, recruit three potential design partners, and learn which deployment controls are essential for adoption in financial services. Nexus's data-sovereignty focus provides a relevant setting to demonstrate the working product and discuss its limits. We also seek introductions to investors interested in local AI infrastructure after customer evidence improves.
