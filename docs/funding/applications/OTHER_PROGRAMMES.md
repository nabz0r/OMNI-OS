# Other programme briefs

These are tailored starting documents for the shortlisted routes. They are not submitted forms. Owner and company fields remain governed by [READINESS.md](../READINESS.md).

## LHoFT

**Request:** incubation fit assessment and introductions to potential fintech design partners.

OMNI is a local memory and request-control layer for AI tools, with a working macOS and Android evaluation MVP. We propose to test a narrow documentation workflow with small fintech engineering or operations teams. Users choose the context a provider receives and can inspect disclosure receipts. Request policies check supported exchanges before they leave the app. We would value LHoFT's help identifying whether this solves a frequent buyer problem, finding a limited number of design partners and understanding procurement expectations in Luxembourg financial services.

The initial trial uses synthetic and approved non-sensitive material. OMNI does not make credit decisions, move money, perform KYC/AML or offer a regulatory certification. Our commercial hypothesis is a paid pilot followed by a maintained organisation subscription. We seek feedback on customer fit before investing in fleet administration.

Attach the one-page brief and [pilot proposal](../PILOT_PROPOSAL.md). Supply actual team and corporate facts. The [services page](https://lhoft.com/services/) establishes incubation and ecosystem support, not guaranteed funding, free membership or admission.

## Technoport

**Request:** pre-incubation assessment, with progression to incubation after business validation.

OMNI has implemented an evaluation product for local AI memory and explicit request controls. The engineering starting point is demonstrable, while the customer segment and business model need validation. We propose a focused programme of workflow interviews, controlled utility comparisons and pilot pricing tests. The principal technical gaps are production distribution, recovery and defensible boundaries for managed deployment. Technoport's concept-validation support would help determine which problem merits further investment.

The [pre-incubation programme](https://technoport.lu/programs/pre-incubation-program) describes a four-month route for individuals or small teams. Attach the business plan, evidence register and proposed milestone schedule. Do not label the concept commercially validated simply because the MVP runs. If customer validation exists outside the repository, add its authentic evidence before assessing the incubation route.

## Techstars

**Programme preference to investigate:** Amsterdam ABN AMRO Future of Finance, followed by Anywhere if its customer and attendance requirements are a better match. Their listing does not establish a live 2027 application window.

OMNI gives users local memory and explicit context-sharing controls across supported AI providers. The evaluation MVP is available for macOS and Android. We plan to start with fintech software teams that repeatedly rebuild project context and need understandable disclosure controls. We seek mentor support for a repeatable buyer workflow, pricing and early sales. The product has engineering evidence; actual commercial metrics must be supplied from company records.

Use the YC core answer bank for product, progress and competition, then add the genuine reason for the chosen programme, relevant mentor/customer needs and founder availability. Review the [investment terms](https://www.techstars.com/investment-terms), including additional MFN dilution and regional exceptions. Do not describe the offer as USD 220,000 for only 5% or assume admission includes a customer contract.

## EIC Accelerator screening memo

**Recommendation:** defer a full application. The [current programme](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en) focuses on market-creating innovation with substantial technical and financing risk, including TRL 6-8 innovation activity. OMNI's repository does not establish a validated relevant environment, independent technical breakthrough assessment or a scale-up financing gap that private capital cannot address.

Before reconsidering: document actual deployment results, define the unresolved research risk, compare alternatives, establish IP rights and show a credible route to market and financing. Assign a TRL only from evidence; a desktop MVP is not automatically TRL 6. Prepare the full proposal only after a qualified pre-screen and verification of the 2027 work programme. No EIC grant or investment belongs in the base cash plan.
