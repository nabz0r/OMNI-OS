## Submission checklist

- Use actual legal names and a monitored business email. Do not infer a founder's legal identity from the `nabz0r` GitHub handle.
- Record the live form's fields, limits and accepted attachments. The prepared answer lengths are drafting aids, not a claim to have inspected authenticated forms.
- Confirm every number as actual, measured result, target or assumption. Include dates and denominators for traction.
- Replace all `OWNER INPUT` items. If the answer is unknown, keep the application unfinished rather than guessing.
- Add verified founder profiles to the deck. Keep roadmap capabilities and security limits visible.
- Reconcile application, deck and workbook figures. Keep grants and equity offers out of actual funding until awarded/closed.
- Resolve overlapping physical attendance and corporate-structure requirements before accepting programmes.
- Review terms and publication scope, submit from the proper account, and retain the receipt, date and exact submitted files.

## Private data room index

Create controlled-access folders for `01-company`, `02-team-and-ip`, `03-finance`, `04-customers`, `05-product-and-security` and `06-applications`. Include incorporation records, cap table, founder agreements, IP assignments, actual bank-backed cash records, monthly revenue reconciliation, signed pilots and product evidence. Grant the minimum necessary access, record who received each version and remove access when the review ends. Do not add identity documents, bank statements, signatures, keys or customer data to this repository.
