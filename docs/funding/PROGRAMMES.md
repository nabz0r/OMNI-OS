## Fit 4 Start screening

The [eligibility page](https://luxinnovation.lu/assess-and-accelerate/fit4start/eligibility) allows projects before incorporation. It requires at least two people, one working full-time and attending programme sessions while developing operations in Luxembourg. Participants must be adults. An existing entity must meet small-enterprise criteria and the five-year age limit, including group assessment where relevant. OMNI's actual team and corporate status are unknown.

The [edition 17 guidelines](https://stlxipuhot.blob.core.windows.net/staging/sharedmedia/luxinnovation/startup-luxembourg-emails/f4s_17_guidelines_website_version.pdf), sections 10-11, set conditional tranches of EUR 50,000/80,000/20,000. They include Luxembourg establishment and a business permit, EUR 15,000 paid-up subscribed capital and evidenced cash for the first tranche. Later funding requires graduation, a EUR 50,000 formal cash capital increase, two full-time employees, eligible expenditure and timely reports. These are edition 17 reference conditions, **not a confirmed offer or edition 18 rules**. The PDF still cites a EUR 200,000 de minimis ceiling; have Luxinnovation confirm the applicable scheme and aggregation rules rather than replacing the number with a generic EU ceiling. A foreign-parent SAFE must not be assumed to satisfy Luxembourg paid-up capital conditions.

The older Guichet downloadable guidelines are explicitly edition 16. Their 2026-2028 dates must not be copied into a new application. No grant receipts enter the financial model by default.

## Programme conflicts and decision rules

- YC [investment rules](https://www.ycombinator.com/deal) require an eligible parent jurisdiction. A future corporate reorganisation could affect Luxembourg aid, IP and tax arrangements. Resolve the structure with the programme and advisers before signing.
- Fit 4 Start has physical participation requirements. Do not promise simultaneous full attendance in Luxembourg and San Francisco. Future YC early decision may be an alternative, subject to the live programme rules.
- LHoFT and Technoport are support routes, not substitutes for contracted sales or a funded runway.
- Nexus booth participation is distinct from a general attendee ticket. Do not buy a separate package merely because a ticket link is prominent.
- Prepare a small number of strong applications. Allocate most founder time to customer interviews, pilot delivery and removal of release blockers.
