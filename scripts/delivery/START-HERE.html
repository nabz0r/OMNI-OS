First-open permissions

This build has an ad-hoc integrity signature, without Developer ID signing or notarization. macOS may require Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Existing vaults may request Keychain authorization through the OS. Read the installation guide before proceeding.

02 / MOBILE

Your space, in your hand.

Android 7+ · ARM64 and x86_64
Native app. Android Keystore protection.

Get the Android APK ↗Install directly on your Android device.
  1. Transfer the APK to your phone.
  2. Open it and allow this installation source.
  3. Connect your provider in guided setup.
Compatibility and updates

Development-signed APK, tested on Android 15 AOSP. A current Android System WebView is required. ARM32-only devices are unsupported. Keep the same signing identity for updates; do not uninstall a personal vault to resolve a signature conflict.
